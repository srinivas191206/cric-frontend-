AERODYNAMICS

What is Swing Force (SF)?

Swing Force (SF) measures the lateral aerodynamic movement of the cricket ball through the air prior to pitching.

Aerodynamic Mechanism

Governed by the pressure differential created across the ball's raised seam and the boundary layer airflow separation.

Measurement Scale

0.1 – 0.5 SF: Subtle late movement
0.6 – 1.2 SF: Pronounced regulation swing
1.3+ SF: High lateral deviation

Right Swing: Curves towards the off side Left Swing: Curves towards the leg side
